## 1. Introduction

Stackforge ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website stackforge.io and use our services.

This Privacy Policy is compliant with:
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**

- **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)** (for California residents)

**Disclaimer:** This is a template Privacy Policy. We recommend having this reviewed by a qualified legal professional in your jurisdiction before deployment.

## 2. Definitions

**Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

**Processing:** Any operation performed on Personal Data (collection, storage, use, analysis, deletion, etc.).

**Data Principal:** The natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

**Data Fiduciary:** The entity (Stackforge) that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data.

**Consent:** Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of the Data Principal's wishes.

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Active Account Data
- **While your account is active:** Indefinitely (until you delete)
- **Email, name, account settings:** Retained as long as your account exists
- **Billing data:** Retained for 7 years (tax compliance)

### 7.2 Saved Projects & Workflows
- **Saved projects:** Retained until you delete or your account is deleted
- **Unsaved workflow data:** Not retained (lost when you leave the page)
- **Shared projects:** Retained until you delete or your account is deleted

### 7.3 After Account Deletion
- **Personal account data:** Deleted within 30 days
- **Billing records:** Retained for 7 years (tax/legal requirement)
- **Usage logs:** Anonymized and retained for 1 year
- **Saved projects:** Deleted permanently
- **Backups:** May persist for up to 90 days before being purged

### 7.4 Cookie Data
- **Session cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Authentication cookies:** Deleted when you log out
- **Analytics cookies:** Retained for up to 2 years (Google Analytics)

## 8. Your Data Rights (DPDP Act Section 8)

### 8.1 Right to Access
You have the right to request access to your Personal Data. We will provide:
- A copy of all Personal Data we hold about you
- Details of how we're Processing your data
- Purposes of Processing
- Recipients of your data

**How to exercise:** Email [privacy@stackforge.io](mailto:privacy@stackforge.io) with "Data Access Request" in subject. Response within 15 days.

### 8.2 Right to Correction
You have the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data.

**How to exercise:** Log in to your account and update your profile. For data you can't access, email [privacy@stackforge.io](mailto:privacy@stackforge.io).

### 8.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
You have the right to request deletion of your Personal Data, except where:
- Required by law (tax records, 7 years)
- Necessary to provide services
- Required for legal claims

**How to exercise:** Log into your account and select "Delete Account." Data deleted within 30 days (backups within 90 days).

### 8.4 Right to Data Portability
You have the right to receive your Personal Data in a structured, commonly-used format and transmit it to another service.

**How to exercise:** Email [privacy@stackforge.io](mailto:privacy@stackforge.io) with "Data Portability Request." We'll provide JSON export of your account data within 30 days.

### 8.5 Right to Grievance Redressal
You have the right to lodge a grievance regarding Processing of your Personal Data.

**How to exercise:** Email [privacy@stackforge.io](mailto:privacy@stackforge.io) with detailed complaint. We'll acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30-45 days.

### 8.6 Right to Withdraw Consent
You can withdraw consent for any optional Processing (marketing emails, analytics) at any time.

**How to exercise:** Click "Unsubscribe" in emails, or update notification preferences in your account settings.

## 9. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your Personal Data:

**Technical:**
- **Encryption in Transit:** TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at Rest:** AES-256 for sensitive data
- **Access Control:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication for staff
- **Data Minimization:** Collect only what's necessary
- **Intrusion Detection:** Automated monitoring for unauthorized access

**Organizational:**
- **Security Training:** Regular training for all staff
- **Data Protection Officer:** Designated DPO for compliance
- **Incident Response:** 24-hour incident response team
- **Backups:** Daily encrypted backups, stored separately

**Third-Party:**
- **Security Audits:** Annual third-party security audits
- **Penetration Testing:** Quarterly pen-testing
- **Vulnerability Management:** Continuous scanning for vulnerabilities

**Limitation:** No security measures are 100% foolproof. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
